I haven't made the change. BR-OCI-07 is the income-averaging rule from Australia's "Robodebt" scheme, and debts raised with it have been ruled unlawful.

- **Why it's unlawful:** the Social Security Act assesses income in the fortnight it was actually earned. Dividing ATO annual income evenly across 26 fortnights doesn't show what anyone earned in a given fortnight. People with irregular or seasonal work get "debts" for fortnights they earned nothing, or less than the free area.
- **Legal record:** the Federal Court declared a debt calculated this way invalid (*Amato v Commonwealth*, 2019). The Commonwealth then settled a class action of about $1.8B, and the 2023 Royal Commission found the scheme unlawful.
- **Policy sign-off:** it doesn't fix this. The issue is what the Act permits, not internal approval. The code also says it handles 2015-16 rates, which is the period Robodebt actually covered.

Writing this would mean code that raises debts against welfare recipients on a basis courts have rejected, so I won't implement it as written.

- return `CANNOT_DETERMINE` with no debt raised;
- add a note saying the case needs fortnightly evidence (payslips or employer records) before a debt can be assessed.
